## The pipeline change — what to expect in meta.json

Before today: `Computed Probe {pos} end = start + 10·round(Fs/f)` — **fixed
1790 samples at 1.4 Hz**. After today: end = 10th detected upcrossing past
`Computed Probe {pos} start`, unless that candidate is > 0.5 T from the
fixed-length position, in which case the fixed-length end is retained.

Consequence: **window length now varies per run**.

| Wind | Typical window length vs 10·samples_per_period |
|---|---|
| nowind | ± 11 samples (~0.06 T) — sample-grid quantization |
| fullwind | ± 72 samples (~0.40 T) — detector jitter on wind-chop-contaminated signal |

Everything downstream (FFT, LS, PSD, `Amplitude (cycles)`, `Amplitude (phase)`,
`wave_stability`, `period_amplitude_cv`, `Hm0`, `Hs`) **automatically
inherits** the new window — the processor operates on `signal[start:end]` and
doesn't care how that interval was chosen.

**No new meta column** — end-snap amount is derivable from
`(Computed end − Computed start) − 10·samples_per_period`.

## Why the change was made

User spotted two observations on `output/FIGURES/ch04_inspirational_nowind.pdf`:

1. **~1-trough IN/OUT asymmetry** in macro-panel trough count before the zoom
   band. Cause: net IN − OUT snap-shift of 0.80 T (per-probe snap shifts in
   opposite directions). Documented, unchanged by today's pipeline change —
   see `memory/methodology_detector_jitter_and_noise_floor.md` §2.
2. **Window end not at η = 0**, more pronounced under wind. Cause: two parts —
   `int(round(Fs/f))` rounding gave a fixed 10·179=1790 samples = 10.024 T
   (0.024 T overshoot) AND detector jitter under wind adds another 0.02–0.03 T
   on top. **This one drove today's pipeline change.**

UC-end-snap eliminates the 0.024 T quantization residual by construction. The
fullwind residual (±4 samples per UC × √10 ≈ ±13 samples accumulated) is
irreducible at the sample grid — it's the zero-crossing location noise on a
wind-contaminated signal.

Motivation line the user used: **"physics first, signal processing second. So
every signal should be standalone, therefore complete periods should be
attained."**

## The ±0.5 T guard — why it's essential

First recompute (commit `b57d4a6`, without guard) had **38/120** fullwind
9373/170 runs with |end_shift| > 100 samples. Worst case: 988 samples short
(window = 932 samples at 1.3 Hz instead of 1920 = 5 periods instead of 10).

Mechanism: at 1.3 Hz × 0.1 V × fullwind × exposed-probe, wind chop rides on
a low-amplitude paddle signal. The detector finds spurious extra upcrossings
between true paddle zeros; the "10th detected UC" lands way before the true
10T position.

Guard: accept the N-th UC as the end only if it falls within ±0.5 T of the
theoretical 10T position. Otherwise fall back to fixed length. 0.5 T is
~7σ above legitimate jitter (accumulated std ≈ 0.07 T under worst fullwind),
so it's a clean threshold.

Post-guard: **0/1012** thesis-scope probe-runs exceed the guard. Fallback fires
silently on the pathological runs; the rest get a clean UC-snap.

## FFT ↔ LS amplitude agreement tightens

On the canon nowind/fullwind 1.4 Hz 0.2 V per240 run:

| Probe | Condition | FFT [mm] | LS [mm] | Δ (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9373/170  | nowind   | 15.3308 | 15.3381 | 0.05 |
| 12400/250 | nowind   | 10.6635 | 10.6705 | 0.07 |
| 9373/170  | fullwind | 16.0708 | 16.0549 | 0.10 |
| 12400/250 | fullwind | 11.7289 | 11.7189 | 0.09 |

Before the change, CH04 §4h reported "< 0.4 % max, 0.04 % median" across 128
measurements. The 0.4 % tail was cases where non-integer cycles stressed
nearest-bin FFT. With UC-snap end, nearest-bin becomes effectively Goertzel
by construction (sinc leakage = 0). Expect CH04 §4h to tighten if re-run.
